Sam Kundu

Sam is a co-founder of Pinnacle Agribusiness Co. Ltd, with over five years of experience in agriculture. He holds a Bachelor of Agribusiness Management from Makerere University, and is currently pursuing a Master of Science in Agricultural and Applied Economics at the same institution.

He has worked with organisations including Hortifresh Fresh Fruits and Vegetables Association, KK Fresh Produce Exporters Limited, Roohaz Agro-Farm Limited, Youths Development Association and Empower East Africa Limited, all shaping his grounding in agribusiness.

Moro is a dynamic agribusiness professional with over four years of experience across agribusiness and the food industry, with strong interests in agritech, digital marketing, small business management and community development.

Eng. Fred Mark Abigaba

Fred Mark holds a BSc. in Agricultural Engineering from Makerere University.
